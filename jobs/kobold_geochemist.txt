Senior Geochemist
About the Role
KoBold explores across several continents and a widening set of commodities. This role is the geochemical capability that travels with those programs.
Reporting to the Principal Geochemist, you will work as a functioning member of exploration teams in North America, Europe, and Africa; planning and running sampling campaigns, on the ground when it matters, and turning results around quickly enough to change what the team does next.
The Principal Geochemist has responsibility for company-wide standards, independent data quality assurance, and global method and technology development. Your responsibility is in the practice, programs, field decisions, interpretation, and the day-to-day geochemical judgement that the exploration teams you work within depend on. You will work closely with data scientists and software engineers to develop new tools and drive an ever deepening analysis of results. You will work to company standards and audit against them in the field. You will be required to prioritize your time effectively across multiple projects, and multiply your reach by training field teams to sample well without you present.
Responsibilities
Demonstrate in all activities a commitment to the highest standards in health and safety, environment, and community relations
Act as the embedded geochemist for exploration programs across North America, Europe, and Africa, joining project teams for the phases where geochemical decisions are being made and providing insight to existing hypotheses and the generation of new hypotheses fast enough to influence exploration decision making
Design geochemical sampling programs fit for the terrain, regolith, and mineral system being tested, including orientation surveys and the choice of sampling medium, horizon, size fraction, spacing, analytical suite, and digestion, across glaciated terrain, deeply weathered tropical profiles, and arid or transported cover
Own the geochemical component of drilling programs: sampling intervals and sample support, preparation protocols, quality control insertion, and real-time review of incoming assays against the geological model
Apply and audit KoBold's geochemical standards and quality control procedures in the field, resolve routine control failures at program level, and escalate systemic or analytical problems to the Principal Geochemist, who retains independent data quality sign-off
In close collaboration with the science team, interpret multi-element geochemical datasets across commodities and deposit types, including lithogeochemical classification, alteration and trace element vectoring, fertility screening, and appropriate treatment of compositional data
Partner with geoscientists, data scientists and software engineers to improve existing tools, and develop new tools
Assess legacy and third-party geochemical datasets during property evaluation and acquisition due diligence, judging quality, comparability, and fitness for purpose, and identifying where re-analysis or re-sampling is required before the data can carry weight
Work alongside geologists, geophysicists, and data scientists to identify, rank, and prioritise targets, making geochemistry a primary input to targeting
Coach and train field geologists and technicians in leading-practice sampling, and contribute to the standard operating procedures and field documentation that let sampling quality hold when no geochemist is on site
Feed field experience back into method and technology development led by the Principal Geochemist, including practical trials of portable XRF, in-field sample preparation, and emerging analytical techniques
Plan and manage a demanding international travel and deployment schedule, in coordination with the Principal Geochemist and project leadership
Qualifications
A great Senior Geochemist candidate will have:
Strong technical knowledge of more than one ore deposit type and comfort moving between mineral systems. Experience across two or more of sediment-hosted stratiform copper, porphyry copper, IOCG, magmatic nickel-copper-PGE sulfide, or LCT pegmatite systems will be considered an advantage
Demonstrated hands-on experience designing and executing surface geochemical programs, including orientation work, in more than one regolith or cover setting
Experience running the geochemical side of drilling programs, from sampling protocol through quality control to interpretation of results as they arrive
Practical command of quality control for sampling programs, including reference material and duplicate design, control charting, and precision and bias assessment, together with working knowledge of the Theory of Sampling
Excellence in generative analysis, from new concepts through to the integration, interpretation, and modeling of large multi-element datasets
Baseline level of proficiency in standard desktop software packages (e.g., ArcGIS or QGIS) and in at least one dedicated geochemical data analysis package (e.g., ioGAS)
The ability to work effectively as a member of teams they do not manage, across cultures, time zones, and technical disciplines, and to earn influence through the quality of their judgement rather than through reporting lines
Previous experience in coaching and training geoscientists of varying experience, educational backgrounds and cultures in leading-practice mineral exploration
Willingness and ability to travel internationally for extended periods, and eligibility to work or obtain the necessary permissions across the regions covered by this role
Demonstrated commitment to high standards of health and safety in the workplace
A minimum of 8 years’ experience in the mineral exploration industry
Intellectual curiosity and eagerness to learn about all aspects of mineral exploration, particularly in the geology domain. Enjoys constantly learning such that you are driving insights through using our tools in exploration and willing to work directly with geologists in the field.
Ability to explain technical problems to and collaborate on solutions with domain experts who are not Geoscientists. A strong communicator who enjoys working with colleagues across the company.
Excitement about joining a fast-growing early-stage company, comfort with a dynamic work environment, and eagerness to take on an evolving range of responsibilities.
Keen not just to build cool technology, but to figure out what technical product to build to best achieve the business objectives of the company.
A valid passport and willingness to travel to exploration sites around the world, especially during active field seasons.
It is helpful but not required to have:
Working knowledge of one programming or scripting language (e.g., Python, R) applied to geochemical data
Knowledge of geostatistics, compositional data analysis, or other statistical methods relevant for analysis of geoscientific data
Experience with portable XRF and in-field sample preparation as auditable, fit-for-purpose analytical capability
Experience in environmental geochemistry, including baseline surveys and waste and tailings characterisation
International experience, particularly in the Central African Copperbelt, or in the pegmatite provinces of central Africa and Namibia
Professional registration or chartership (e.g., CGeol, FAusIMM, EurGeol, SACNASP)
Working proficiency in French 
Note: Candidates whose background is in exploration geology with a strong geochemical specialisation, and who want to work as a geochemist across a global portfolio rather than a single project, are encouraged to apply.
